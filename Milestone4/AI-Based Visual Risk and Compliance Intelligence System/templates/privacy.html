1. Platform Overview & Principles

Personal Decision Support Governance

The AI-Based Visual Risk and Compliance Intelligence System functions as a secure decision-support engine. By design, the platform creates an adaptive digital model of your lifestyle and commitments to simulate outcomes and forecast risk before you act. Because our intelligence platform depends on sensitive personal vectors, we enforce a strict Privacy-by-Design philosophy across all processing pipelines.

2. Telemetry & Data We Model

Pillars of the Digital Twin

To evaluate risk indices and simulate your future scenarios, the system stores inputs under the following pillars:

Financial Activities Recorded income intervals, expense categories, recurring liabilities, and budget limits used to forecast liquidity stress and compliance exposure.
Study & Academic Patterns Logged study durations, syllabus progress, milestone dates, and subject performance metrics used for GPA simulation.
Lifestyle & Physical Telemetry Sleep schedules, active physical windows, and recovery milestones used to calculate cognitive fatigue and burnout guardrails.
Habits & Compliance Rules Custom boundary constraints set by you (e.g., maximum daily leisure spending, minimum rest hours, mandatory study slots).

3. How AI & Predictive Models Use Data

Machine Learning Boundaries

Our algorithms generate statistical predictions regarding future outcomes based on historical patterns. We commit to:

  • Non-Automated Detriment: System alerts are exclusively advisory. The engine serves trade-off simulations and warnings to support you, never making unalterable decisions on your behalf.
  • No Cross-Tenant Training: Your private financial logs and personal study habits are never ingested into global generative foundation models accessible to other users.
  • Transparent Scoring: Risk ratings displayed across your dashboards cite specific contributing factors (e.g., cashflow velocity or study deficits).

4. Security, Storage & Encryption

Data Fortress Standards

All communication between your browser and our platform is protected with TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols[cite: 1]. Database entries representing your Digital Twin vectors are partitioned and encrypted with AES-256 bit keys. Regular security audits verify that behavioral telemetry remains isolated from administrative views[cite: 1].

You retain complete ownership over every data point used to formulate your Digital Twin. You can:

Freeze Telemetry Sync temporarily.
Delete Account & Models permanently.
